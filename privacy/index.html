Privacy, in plain language.

Last updated: 27 August 2026.

Wordlist Arcade is designed so we do not need your identity or your students’ data.

What stays on your device

The vocabulary draft you type is saved in your browser’s local storage so it is there when you return. You can remove it at any time with “Clear list,” by clearing this site’s browser data, or by using private browsing.

What is inside a shared link

When you copy a class link, the list name, words, and meanings are compressed into the part of the URL after the #. Browsers do not send that hash fragment to our web server. Anyone you give the link to can read and play that list, so do not put names, grades, sensitive student information, or confidential material in a list.

What we collect

No account, cookies, advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or third-party runtime services are used. The hosting service may process standard short-lived web server logs, such as an IP address, request time, and browser information, for security and reliable delivery.

Children

The product does not ask children for personal information. Teachers should share only classroom content and supervise use according to their school’s policies.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact the site operator through Sociobot. You can return to Wordlist Arcade at any time.
